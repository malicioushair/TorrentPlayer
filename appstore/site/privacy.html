Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 17, 2026 · Applies to the TorrentPlayer app

This Privacy Policy describes how TorrentPlayer (“the App,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use the App. TorrentPlayer is developed by Dmitry Vorobyev.

Short version: we do not run accounts on our own servers, and we do not use analytics or advertising trackers. Data stays on your Mac unless you use optional subtitle services or BitTorrent networking, which involve third parties by design.

1. Information we do not collect

We do not operate our own backend that collects personal information from the App. In particular, we do not:

  • Create or require a TorrentPlayer account
  • Collect your name, email address, or payment details through the App
  • Use third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs in the App
  • Sell, rent, or share your personal information with data brokers

Purchases made through the Mac App Store are processed by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy.

2. Information stored on your device

The App stores preferences and working data locally on your Mac, including:

  • App settings such as download folder location, interface language, appearance options, and subtitle preferences
  • Optional subtitle credentials you enter (for example OpenSubtitles username/password or a SubDL API key)
  • Optional media identifiers you provide or that are derived for subtitle matching (for example an IMDb ID)
  • Downloaded media and torrent resume data needed to continue transfers and play files

This information remains on your device unless you choose features that send it to a third-party service described below.

3. Subtitle providers (optional)

If you use subtitle search or download, the App contacts third-party providers you enable or that ship with default API configuration, currently including:

  • OpenSubtitles (opensubtitles.com)
  • SubDL (subdl.com)

Depending on the provider and your settings, a request may include:

  • Account credentials or API keys you configured
  • Preferred subtitle language
  • Video-related metadata used for matching, such as file name, file size, content hash, IMDb ID, and season/episode numbers when available

Those providers process the request under their own privacy policies. We do not control how they store or use the information they receive.

4. BitTorrent networking

When you open a torrent or magnet link, the App uses the BitTorrent protocol to exchange data with peers and communicate with trackers or other discovery mechanisms. As with any BitTorrent client, participants in a swarm can observe network information such as your IP address and the torrents you participate in.

We do not operate a torrent index or content catalog. You are responsible for only accessing media you own or are authorized to use.

5. Network diagnostics visible to your system

Like other networked apps, general connection metadata (for example IP address and timestamps) may be processed by your operating system, network provider, or DNS resolver when the App makes internet requests. That processing is outside our control.

6. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through optional third-party features, contact us and we will help you understand how to remove related local data from the device.

7. Data retention and your choices

  • You can clear or change local credentials and settings inside the App
  • You can disable or avoid subtitle provider features
  • You can stop BitTorrent activity by closing transfers or not opening torrent/magnet links
  • You can uninstall the App to remove the application; delete related local files and preferences if you want residual data removed

8. International users

Third-party services you choose to use may process data in other countries. Their terms and privacy policies govern that processing.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Effective date” at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
